The MPI polling regression was resolved by rechecking task stickiness at every backoff: sticky tasks park with Julia `sleep`; nonsticky tasks use a short OS sleep and yield. A 4096-poll budget avoids repeated millisecond timer parking during short handshakes. Final Intel synchronous eight-region results are about 10–11 ms, versus 44–47 ms under unconditional timer parking. Ready-receive allocations remain exactly 37,946 / 1,799,136 bytes per 1000 receives before and after, warmed ten times and taking minima over seven runs. Focused starvation and four-rank forwarding pass, and the exact CI layouts (two/four ranks, two default plus one interactive thread) pass 72 assertions. See [mpi-poll-latency-source.json](mpi-poll-latency-source.json) and [mpi-ci-layout-status.json](mpi-ci-layout-status.json).
